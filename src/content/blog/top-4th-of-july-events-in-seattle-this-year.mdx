As proud members of the Seattle community, we always look forward to the 4th of July - and the Puget Sound area has no shortage of ways to celebrate. Here are a few of our favorite local traditions.

## Seafair Summer Fourth

One of the region's biggest draws is the free fireworks show at Gas Works Park, typically kicking off around 10:20 PM alongside live entertainment and food vendors. Just leave the alcohol, glass bottles, and pets at home.

## Grand Old Fourth of July on Bainbridge Island

A ferry ride away, Bainbridge Island puts on a full day of celebration - a pancake breakfast, a classic car show, a street fair, and fireworks in the evening.

## Tacoma Freedom Fair and Airshow

Tacoma's celebration brings together six music stages, food booths, and beer gardens, capped off with a fireworks display around 10:10 PM.

## Urban Adventure Quest

For a more active way to spend the holiday, this scavenger hunt starts at the Space Needle and combines sightseeing with local history across the city.

## Fremont Art Walk

Held monthly from 6 to 9 PM, this free event showcases local painters, sculptors, musicians, and vendors - a nice low-key complement to the bigger fireworks shows.

## Beyond the Big City

Smaller communities have their own traditions worth the drive, including Oak Harbor's fireworks, street fair, and parade on Whidbey Island, and the Maxwelton 4th of July Parade with its games and community spirit.
