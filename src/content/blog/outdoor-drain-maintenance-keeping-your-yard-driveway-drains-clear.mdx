Seattle's rain is a fact of life, and it puts real strain on the drains that keep your yard and driveway from flooding. When those drains clog, the results go beyond a puddle - think erosion, foundation seepage, and cracked pavement.

## What Clogs Outdoor Drains

Leaves, dirt, pine needles, and loose gravel accumulate in yard and driveway drains year-round, but the problem gets worse in fall and winter when heavy rainfall washes more debris into the system at once.

## Why It's Worth Staying Ahead Of

Neglected outdoor drains create more than an inconvenience. Standing water can freeze into hazardous icy patches, soil erosion damages landscaping, and water that backs up near your foundation promotes mold growth indoors. Over time, the added stress can crack driveways and patios, and debris that's allowed to harden becomes much more difficult to remove.

## Warning Signs to Watch For

Keep an eye out for pooling water near your driveway or patio after rain, drainage that seems sluggish even after a light shower, water overflowing from drain grates, foul odors coming from outdoor drains, and visible debris piling up in the grates themselves.

## DIY Maintenance

A little regular attention goes a long way: clear leaves and debris from grates weekly during fall, flush drains with a garden hose to check flow, use a drain snake on minor blockages, and install protective covers over grates to catch debris before it enters the pipe.

## When to Call a Professional

For compacted dirt, tree roots, or silt that's built up over multiple seasons, a garden hose won't cut it. Our team uses [hydro jetting](/hydrojetting/) equipment to clear outdoor drains completely and inspect for any structural damage hiding underneath.

## A Season-by-Season Approach

Each season brings its own challenge - fall means leaf accumulation, winter brings freezing backups, spring's heavy rain intensifies existing clogs, and summer's dry heat hardens whatever debris is left inside the pipe. Regular raking, root trimming, and an annual professional inspection are the best way to keep your yard and driveway drains clear no matter the season.
