We love hearing from customers about their experience, and Sharon S. from Kirkland, WA recently shared hers after a visit that covered several jobs at once.

## What we did

Sharon's project wasn't a single simple fix - our technicians went under her house to complete a gas conversion, installed her new stove, and replaced all the flex pipes running to her toilets. It's the kind of visit where a lot of different pieces have to come together correctly, from gas line work to fixture connections.

## In her words

"Gene Johnson Heating, Cooling, Plumbing and Electrical came on time to my house. They went under my house to do a gas conversion and installed my stove; they also replaced all the flex pipes to all toilets. They are very processional and knowledgeable; I highly recommend them!"

## Why it matters

Sharon's experience reflects what we aim for on every job: showing up on time, communicating clearly, and handling the work - whether it's a gas conversion, an appliance install, or plumbing supply lines - with the same level of care. Projects that touch gas lines and water supply lines both require licensed expertise, and we're glad Sharon felt confident in the work from start to finish.
