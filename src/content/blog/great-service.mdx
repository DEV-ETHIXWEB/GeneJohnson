## An Adventure in Finding the Right Fit

Not every job goes exactly as planned, and Annette's kitchen sink replacement was a good example. What should have been a straightforward swap turned into a search for a sink that would actually fit the opening left behind by the old one - not every replacement sink is a drop-in match for what came before.

## Richard Made It Work

Annette worked with our technician Richard throughout the project. "Richard was great to work with my kitchen sink replacement," she told us. "This turned out to be a challenge simply finding a sink to fit the opening left by the old one. All in all quite an adventure but the end result is fantastic."

## Why the Right Fit Matters

A kitchen sink opening isn't always a standard size, especially in older homes or after a previous remodel. Getting it wrong means gaps, leaks, or a sink that just doesn't sit right - which is exactly the kind of problem a licensed plumber is equipped to solve. Richard's persistence in tracking down the right sink turned a potential headache into a finished kitchen Annette is happy with.

If your next project involves matching a new fixture to an odd or older opening, our team has seen it before. Whether you need a full [kitchen sink install](/faucets-fixtures-sinks/) or a simpler faucet swap, we'll make sure the fit is right the first time.
