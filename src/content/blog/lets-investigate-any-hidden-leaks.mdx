Broken seals, aging fixtures, and damaged pipes can all cause leaks that stay hidden behind walls or under floors for far longer than you'd expect. Catching them early is the difference between a quick repair and a costly restoration project.

## Wall Damage

Paint or wallpaper that starts blistering or peeling for no obvious reason can be a sign of moisture trapped between surfaces. Water breaks down the adhesive bonds that hold paint and wallpaper in place, and that damage often shows up long before you'd notice a leak any other way.

## Mold and Mildew

Mold or mildew growing on walls or ceilings, especially in areas without an obvious source of moisture, is a strong sign of a leak somewhere behind the surface. Pipes running through walls create exactly the damp, dark conditions mold needs to thrive.

## Persistent Odors

A musty, earthy, wet, or sour smell that lingers even after cleaning usually points to stagnant water from a leaking pipe somewhere nearby.

## Rising Water Bills

If your water bill climbs noticeably without a rate change or any increase in your household's actual usage, that's often one of the clearest signs of a hidden leak.

## Structural Damage

Sagging, cracking, bubbling, or discoloration in walls, floors, or ceilings signals prolonged water exposure and, left unaddressed, can eventually become a structural safety issue.
