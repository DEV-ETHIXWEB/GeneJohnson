Valentine's Day comes with enough pressure without your home's heating and cooling system deciding to act up. A little attention beforehand goes a long way toward keeping things comfortable, whatever your plans look like this year.

## Watch for Temperature Swings

If you find yourself alternating between shivering under the comforter and sweating under the sheets, that's not romantic - it's a sign your HVAC system needs professional attention. Left alone, an inconsistent system wastes energy while making your home less comfortable than it should be.

## Emergencies Don't Check the Calendar

Heating and cooling systems have an uncanny habit of failing on holidays and special occasions. That's exactly why Gene Johnson offers 24/7 emergency service - if your system picks Valentine's Day to break down, a technician can still get to you the same day.

## Don't Forget the Filter

Filters quietly accumulate dirt, dust, allergens, hair, grease, and all sorts of other buildup that ends up circulating through your home. Regular filter replacement and vent cleaning keep your system running efficiently and your air genuinely clean.

## Comfort Isn't Just for Couples

Valentine's Day doesn't have to be about a relationship at all - sometimes the best gift is a comfortable, well-maintained home to relax in on your own terms. Either way, a home that's actually pleasant to be in supports whatever kind of day you're planning.
