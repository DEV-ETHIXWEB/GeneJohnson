It's tempting to shrug off a drain that's a little slower than usual or makes an odd gurgling noise now and then. But those small symptoms are early warnings, and ignoring them tends to cost a lot more than addressing them early.

## Why drains start failing in the first place

Obstructions build up gradually inside pipes - grease, food debris, soap residue, and hair are the most common culprits. In older Seattle-area homes, mineral scale and tree root intrusion add to the problem, narrowing pipes further with every passing month.

## What gurgling is actually telling you

That bubbling sound is trapped air being pushed back through standing water, usually because a partial blockage or a clogged vent pipe is disrupting normal airflow. If more than one drain gurgles at the same time - especially toilets - it's often a sign the main sewer line itself is backing up, which is a serious problem that needs immediate attention, not a minor inconvenience.

## What happens if you wait

- A minor buildup becomes a complete blockage
- Standing water starts to damage walls, floors, and cabinetry
- Trapped moisture invites mold and mildew growth
- Ongoing strain weakens pipe joints and creates new leaks
- Multiple slow or gurgling drains can point to a failing main line

## What you can do yourself

Hot water rinses, baking soda and vinegar treatments, drain snakes, and strainers all help with minor, early-stage issues. Skip the chemical drain cleaners - they're hard on pipes and the environment, and they rarely solve the underlying problem anyway.

## Why professional service works better long-term

Licensed plumbers use augers and [hydrojetting](/hydrojetting/) to fully clear hardened grease, hair, and mineral deposits from the inside of the pipe - not just punch a temporary hole through the clog the way DIY tools often do.

## Preventing the problem in the first place

Keep grease and oils out of kitchen drains, install strainers in sinks and tubs, run hot water after each use, skip "flushable" wipes entirely, and schedule a professional drain inspection periodically - especially if your home is older. If a drain has been slow or gurgling for more than a few days, it's worth having it looked at before it becomes an emergency.
