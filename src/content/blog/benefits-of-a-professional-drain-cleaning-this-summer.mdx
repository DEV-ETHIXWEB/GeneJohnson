Every day, ordinary household activity sends food, hair, minerals, and grease into your home's drainage system. Over time, that buildup turns into slow drains, backups, and eventually, expensive repairs.

## Why Summer Raises the Risk

Summer means more people home more often - more cooking, more laundry, more showers - which means more everyday debris working its way into your pipes. That extra usage raises the odds of a clog showing up right in the middle of your summer plans.

## Why Store-Bought Products Fall Short

Over-the-counter drain products might clear a surface-level clog, but they can't remove the deep mineral and grease buildup clinging to the inside walls of your pipes. As that buildup thickens over time, it traps even more debris, setting the stage for a backup down the line.

## What Professional Cleaning Actually Does

A professional drain cleaning clears out all of the debris and buildup throughout the entire drain system, not just the visible clog. It's the kind of preventive maintenance that can ensure long life for your drains and an easy, backup-free, stress-free summer for your family.
