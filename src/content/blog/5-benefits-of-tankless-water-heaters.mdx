If you're weighing a water heater replacement, it's worth understanding what sets [tankless water heaters](/tankless-water-heaters/) apart from the traditional tank models most homes have had for decades.

## Energy Efficiency

Tankless water heaters only heat water as it's needed, rather than continuously maintaining the temperature of a full tank around the clock. That on-demand approach cuts down on wasted energy and can meaningfully lower your annual utility costs.

## Continuous Hot Water

Traditional tanks have a limited supply - run through it, and you're waiting for a refill. Tankless systems provide hot water continuously, so you're not rationing showers or timing the dishwasher around how much is left in the tank.

## A Longer Lifespan

Traditional water heaters typically last 10 to 15 years. Tankless units, with proper maintenance, can operate for up to 20 years - stretching out the time before you're facing another replacement.

## Easier Upkeep

Because tankless systems don't hold standing water, they avoid the sediment buildup that plagues traditional tanks over time. In hard water areas, periodic cleaning is usually enough to keep them running well, which means less money spent on service calls over the life of the unit.

## A Smaller Footprint

A traditional tank typically measures around 60 inches tall by 24 inches wide. Tankless units take up roughly half that space, which matters a lot in homes where utility space is tight.
