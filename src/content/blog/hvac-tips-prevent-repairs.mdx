A little monthly attention to your HVAC system goes a long way toward avoiding an expensive repair bill down the road. Here are the habits worth building into your routine.

## Check the Filter

Your air filter affects your home's air quality, your system's energy efficiency, and how well it cools. A dirty filter can restrict airflow badly enough to cause the system to freeze up or break down entirely, sometimes leading to water damage on top of the original problem. Check it monthly: reusable filters can be cleaned with a sponge, soap, and water, while standard disposable filters should generally be replaced about once a month, especially if your air conditioner is running continuously through the summer.

## Schedule Annual Tune-Ups

Professional maintenance is one of the easiest, most cost-effective things you can do to prevent expensive repairs. A technician cleans components that affect performance and longevity, and catches small problems while they're still small - well before they turn into a system-down emergency.

## Clean Up Around the Outdoor Unit

Your condenser needs room to breathe. Trim back trees, branches, or hedges that have grown in close, and make sure the unit is sitting level - an unlevel unit can interfere with proper refrigerant flow and lubrication inside the system. Beyond that:

- Clear away any leaves or debris that have collected around or on top of the unit
- Wipe down the coils
- Clean the fan blades to cut down on operating noise
- Gently clear dirt from between the fins without bending them

These are small tasks, but skipping them is one of the most common reasons HVAC systems fail earlier than they should.
