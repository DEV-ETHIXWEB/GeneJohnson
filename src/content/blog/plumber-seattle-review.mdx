Sometimes the small details are what a customer remembers most. That was the case with a recent review from Clint S., who called us out for a leak in a copper pipe joint.

## The Job

Copper pipe leaks are a common repair, but they often require torch work to properly re-solder a joint — which means safety precautions matter just as much as the repair itself.

## What Clint Noticed

Clint's technician, Woody, worked through the job despite some payment complications along the way, and Clint noted he was "very understanding" about it. What really stuck with him, though, was that Woody remembered to cover the smoke alarm before using his torch — a small step that shows real attention to detail and respect for the home.

## Why This Kind of Feedback Matters

We train our technicians to treat every home like it's their own, and that includes the little things nobody would notice unless something went wrong. Clint's review is a good reminder that those details are exactly what separates a good repair from a great one.
