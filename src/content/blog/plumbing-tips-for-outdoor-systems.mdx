Being proactive about your home's outdoor systems in spring can save you from bigger problems once summer rolls around. Here's where to start.

## Test Your Outdoor Faucets

Turn each outdoor faucet on and off and watch for dripping. A drip can mean anything from a busted pipe to a worn-out washer, and left alone it leads to water damage and a higher water bill than you'd expect from something so small.

## Clear Out the Eavestroughs

Gutters that collected debris over the winter can create blockages that stop water from draining the way it's supposed to. Clearing them out before the heavier spring rains arrive helps prevent flooding and water damage around your foundation.

## Inspect Your Hoses

Cold winter temperatures can leave hoses with holes and cracks you won't notice until you turn the water on. A quick inspection before the first real use of the season can prevent a surprising amount of wasted water and a higher utility bill.

## Run Your Sprinkler System

Turn the sprinklers on briefly and watch each zone. Problem heads that are clogged, misaligned, or broken often show themselves right away, and catching them early means a quick fix instead of a summer of dead patches in your lawn.
