Here's a quick one worth remembering next time you're standing over the sink deciding whether something can go down the disposal.

## What to Keep Out

Corn silk, celery, bones, and grease are common culprits behind disposal damage and jams. Corn silk and celery are stringy enough to wrap around the blades, bones are simply too hard for most disposals to handle, and grease solidifies inside the unit and the pipes beyond it.

## The Simple Rule

When in doubt, throw it out. Garbage disposals have real limitations, and it's always cheaper to toss a questionable scrap in the trash than to pay for a repair — or a full replacement — down the line.

## Keep It Running Smoothly

A little caution at the sink goes a long way toward avoiding clogs, jams, and mechanical failures in your [garbage disposal](/garbage-disposals/).
