No plumbing issue is too small to take seriously - including the ones you can't even see. At Gene Johnson, we handle everything from a slow drip to a full leak detection job, and we'd rather catch a small problem early than get called out for a burst pipe later.

## Skip the Chemical Drain Cleaners

It's tempting to reach for a bottle of drain cleaner at the first sign of a slow sink, but those products can cause permanent damage to your pipes, sinks, or bathtub. What looks like a quick fix can end up corroding the very pipes you're trying to save.

## Preventing Clogs the Right Way

A little routine care goes a long way. Clean your drains regularly, and use a refuse trap before draining water from dishwashing - it catches the food particles and grease that would otherwise build up inside your pipes over time.

## The Leaks You Can't See

Not every leak announces itself with a puddle. Some hide behind walls or under slabs, and the first sign might just be unexpectedly low water pressure. That's where professional [leak detection](/slab-leak-repair/) tools come in - the equipment to pinpoint a hidden leak without tearing your home apart guessing.

## Don't Wait on a Small Leak

A small leak rarely stays small. Left alone, it can escalate into a burst pipe, and what would have been a simple repair turns into a major, expensive one. If something seems off with your water pressure or you notice a drip you can't explain, [schedule now](/schedule-now/) rather than waiting to see if it gets worse.
