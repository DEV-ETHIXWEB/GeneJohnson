Hiring a plumber isn't a decision to rush. Taking a few minutes to vet a prospective plumber up front helps ensure you get the best value for your money and quality, professional work when the job is done.

## Do They Offer Emergency Service?

Plumbing crises rarely happen at a convenient time. Before you need one, ask whether the plumber operates outside normal business hours and offers true emergency service - not every company does, and finding out at 2 a.m. during a burst pipe is the worst possible time to learn the answer is no.

## Are They Licensed and Experienced?

Ask to see proof of licensing and accreditation before any work begins. Experience matters too: a more experienced plumber is generally better equipped to handle tough or unusual plumbing situations, so it's worth asking how long they've been in business and what kinds of jobs they typically handle.

## What Will It Actually Cost?

Get clarity on rates and service call charges before the visit, not after. Asking upfront protects you from being stuck with a large, unforeseen bill once the work is already done.

## The Bottom Line

A licensed, experienced plumber will typically cost more than an unlicensed handyman, but that cost buys real protection - work that meets code, holds up to inspection, and keeps your home and family safe. For Bothell homeowners weighing a few quotes, these three questions are a quick way to separate a solid choice from a risky one.
