A kitchen remodel doesn't have to mean tearing out every cabinet and starting from scratch. A handful of targeted changes can shift the entire feel of the room — here are five worth considering.

## Swap in Open Shelving

Trading traditional upper cabinets for open shelving is one of the fastest ways to modernize a kitchen's look, and it makes everyday cookware and dinnerware significantly easier to reach and arrange.

## Replace the Kitchen Sink

Your sink gets used more than almost any other fixture in the house, so it's worth getting right. Because a sink installation involves both plumbing and countertop work, professional installation is the way to go — it ensures there won't be any messy leaks or hidden problems down the line.

## Update the Lighting

Lighting is one of the most overlooked elements in a kitchen remodel, but new fixtures can dramatically change how a space looks and functions, often for a fraction of the cost of a full renovation.

## Add Smarter Storage

Small additions like a built-in spice rack or wine storage add both practicality and personality to a kitchen without requiring a major overhaul.

## Upgrade Your Plumbing

While you're at it, consider adding a [water filtration system](/water-filtration-systems/), a new [garbage disposal](/garbage-disposals/), or an updated dishwasher line. These upgrades improve both the functionality of your kitchen and the quality of the water and food that come out of it.
