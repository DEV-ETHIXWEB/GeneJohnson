Most homeowners assume water only flows one way: from the city main into the house. Under the wrong conditions, it can reverse - a phenomenon called backflow - and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

## What backflow actually is

Backflow happens when a change in water pressure - a main break, a sudden spike in demand, or backpressure from something like an irrigation system or a boiler - causes water to flow backward through your plumbing instead of forward. Instead of clean water reaching your taps, contaminated water can be pulled into the supply line.

## Why it matters more in Seattle

Older infrastructure and our persistently wet climate both raise the risk here. If backflow occurs, contamination can include bacteria, chemicals, fertilizers, or waste - a genuine health hazard, particularly for kids, elderly family members, or anyone with a compromised immune system.

## Common sources of risk around your home

- Lawn irrigation and sprinkler systems
- Hose bibs without vacuum breakers
- Boilers and hydronic heating systems
- Swimming pools and hot tubs
- Wells or other auxiliary water sources

Any of these can create the pressure conditions that pull water backward if they aren't properly protected.

## How prevention devices work

Backflow prevention relies on dedicated devices - atmospheric vacuum breakers, pressure vacuum breakers, double-check valve assemblies, and reduced pressure zone assemblies, depending on the risk level involved. Which one you need depends on your home's specific plumbing layout, which is why installation should be assessed by a professional rather than guessed at.

## Testing isn't optional

In many Seattle-area cases, annual certification of backflow prevention devices is required, and for good reason - these devices have to work perfectly every single time they're needed, not most of the time.

## Signs something's wrong

Discolored water, odd tastes or smells, unexpected pressure changes, visible leaks near a backflow device, or a failed test notice from the city are all reasons to get it checked promptly.
