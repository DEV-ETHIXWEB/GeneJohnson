A burst pipe in the middle of the night, a flooded toilet just before guests arrive, or a leak under the kitchen sink can turn your day upside down in minutes. While a licensed plumber should always handle the real fix, a well-stocked emergency kit lets you limit the damage while you wait for [emergency plumbing](/emergency-plumbing/) help to arrive.

## Essential Tools

Start with the basics: an adjustable wrench and a pipe wrench for tightening or loosening fittings, Teflon tape for sealing threaded connections, a sturdy plunger, a utility knife, and a reliable flashlight. Together, these let you shut off water, tighten a loose connection, or work safely in a dark crawlspace or under a cabinet.

## Quick-Fix Supplies

Keep pipe repair clamps, epoxy putty, a few buckets, rags, duct tape, and rubber patches on hand. None of these are permanent fixes, but they can contain a leak long enough to prevent serious water damage while you arrange for a repair.

## Clog Response

Stock a dedicated toilet plunger and a separate sink plunger - using the right tool for the job makes a real difference. A small hand-crank drain snake is worth adding too. A plunger often clears a clogged toilet, but having a snake within reach can save the day when it doesn't.

## Safety Gear

Work gloves, protective eyewear, a face mask, and knee pads protect you from the mess and the strain of an unexpected repair, especially if you're working in a cramped space.

## The Most Important Step

Before anything else, know where your main water shut-off valve is and label it clearly. This single piece of knowledge can prevent a minor leak from turning into major water damage. Keep a laminated card with shut-off instructions stored right alongside your kit so anyone in the household can respond quickly, even if you're not home.

## Know Your Limits

A kit is for triage, not repair. Slab leaks, sewer backups, and major burst-pipe damage need a licensed plumber's tools and training. When something is beyond a temporary fix, [contact us](/contact/) - we're available for the emergencies your kit was never meant to solve.
