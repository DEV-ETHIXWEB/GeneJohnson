As temperatures drop, heating bills tend to climb right along with them. The good news is that most of the biggest savings come from simple, low-cost habits rather than expensive upgrades. Here's where to start.

## Get a Furnace Tune-Up

A furnace tune-up can help identify smaller problems before they turn into bigger ones, and it ensures your system is converting energy as efficiently as possible - which shows up directly on your bill.

## Reverse Your Ceiling Fans

Running ceiling fans in reverse during the colder months helps circulate warm air that naturally rises back down into the room, so your furnace doesn't have to work as hard to keep things comfortable.

## Seal Up Your Doors

Installing adjustable thresholds and weather stripping around exterior doors closes off the gaps where warm air quietly escapes all winter long.

## Address Your Windows

Window film is a budget-friendly alternative to full replacement, and caulking any cracks in the frame helps keep the seal airtight without a major investment.

## Manage Your Chimney

If your fireplace isn't in use, keep the damper closed, and consider a chimney pillow to stop warm air from escaping straight up and out of the house.

## Let the Sun Do the Work

On clear days, leave your curtains open and let sunlight warm your home naturally - it costs nothing and can make a noticeable difference in how hard your furnace has to run.
