Every so often a review comes in that captures exactly what we're aiming for on every job. This one came from Layna E. of Seattle, after we completed a re-piping project that included replacing her waterline from the city connection.

## The Job

Re-piping is not a small undertaking — it involves running new supply lines through the home and, in Layna's case, excavating the front yard to replace the waterline coming in from the street. That's the kind of project where communication and cleanup matter just as much as the plumbing itself.

## What She Had to Say

Layna praised technicians Brandon and Josh for being "clean, quick, detailed," and for taking the time to actually address her concerns as the work progressed. Despite the scope of the excavation, the crew handled the front-yard digging professionally and restored the area afterward, rather than leaving her with a torn-up yard.

She also had kind words for Kristen on our office staff, who provided courteous phone support over the weekend when Layna needed to check in.

## Why It Matters to Us

Layna closed her review by saying she'd make us her first call for any future service needs — which is the outcome we aim for on every [repiping](/repiping/) job, not just the ones that get reviewed.
