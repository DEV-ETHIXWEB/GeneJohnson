The best time to service a boiler is before you actually need it working at full capacity. Taking care of a few key maintenance items during the warmer months can help you avoid a breakdown right when Seattle's coldest weather hits.

## Bleed the Radiators

Trapped air inside a radiator interferes with heat circulation and makes your system work harder than it needs to. A simple way to check is to feel the radiator itself - if it's hot on the bottom and cool near the top, that's a sign air needs to be bled out so heat can circulate properly.

## Check the Condensation Pipe

The condensation pipe carries moisture away from the boiler to an outdoor drain, and if it freezes, it can shut the whole system down. A technician can wrap it in foam pipe insulation or heating tape to keep it protected through the coldest stretches of winter.

## Clean the Boiler

Dirt and debris build up inside a boiler over time, reducing efficiency and potentially blocking gas outlets. A technician uses specialized equipment to vacuum out buildup from the internal components and openings, restoring the system to proper working order.

Taking care of these three items now - bleeding the radiators, protecting the condensation pipe, and cleaning the unit - goes a long way toward getting through winter without an unexpected outage.
