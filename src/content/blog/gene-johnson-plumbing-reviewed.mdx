## A Flood, and Homeowners on the Other Side of the World

Few situations are more stressful than discovering a flooded basement in a home that isn't yours. That's exactly what happened to Michael, who was house-sitting when the basement flooded - with the homeowners in Cambodia at the time and unable to help in person.

## Responding Within the Hour

Michael reached out to us, and our team responded within the hour. Beyond stopping the immediate water problem, we made sure he had everything he needed going forward, including the information required to file a claim for the water damage through the homeowners' insurance - all coordinated with owners who were halfway around the world.

"Talk about excellent service," Michael told us. "These guys deserve it."

## Why This Kind of Response Matters

A flooded basement doesn't wait for a convenient time, and it doesn't care whether the people who live there are reachable. Situations like Michael's are exactly why we run [emergency plumbing](/emergency-plumbing/) service around the clock - because water damage compounds fast, and having someone show up within the hour can be the difference between a manageable cleanup and a much bigger insurance claim.

If you're a homeowner, a renter, or watching a property for someone else and a plumbing emergency hits, don't wait it out. [Contact us](/contact/) any time, day or night.
