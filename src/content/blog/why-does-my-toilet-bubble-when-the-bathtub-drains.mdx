If your toilet gurgles or bubbles every time the bathtub empties, your plumbing is trying to tell you something. That "glug-glug" sound is air escaping somewhere it shouldn't, and in older Seattle homes with irregular pipe runs, it's a fairly common symptom - but it's worth figuring out the cause rather than ignoring it.

## The usual suspects

**Trapped air in the pipes.** As water rushes through the drain lines, it can create air pockets that get forced out through the nearest opening - often your toilet.

**A drainage blockage.** Hair, soap scum, food particles, and mineral deposits narrow the pipe over time. Once flow gets turbulent enough, it can suck air backward through the toilet's trap.

**A blocked vent pipe.** Every drain system relies on vent pipes to equalize air pressure. If the roof vent is blocked by debris, leaves, or even an animal nest, water flowing through the drains creates a vacuum that pulls air - and noise - back through your fixtures.

**Improper installation.** If you've had recent renovation or plumbing work done, incorrect venting, insufficient pipe slope, or a poor connection can all produce this exact symptom.

## What you can try first

A plunger or drain snake can clear a minor clog, and cleaning out the P-trap under a sink or tub can remove trapped debris. Sometimes a simple baking soda and vinegar flush is enough to break up light buildup. If you're comfortable getting on the roof, a quick visual check of the vent pipe opening for obvious blockage is worth doing too.

## When to call in a professional

If the gurgling doesn't clear up, comes with a foul odor, or you notice slow drainage at more than one fixture, it's time to stop guessing. Those are signs the problem is further down the line than a simple trap or vent issue.
