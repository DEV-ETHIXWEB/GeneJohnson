As the weather turns, a handful of plumbing problems tend to show up right on schedule for Seattle homeowners. Catching them early with a bit of preventive maintenance is a lot cheaper than dealing with the aftermath.

## Main Sewer Drain Clogs

Toilets that gurgle after the washing machine runs, or wet spots appearing near basement floor drains, are early warning signs of a main sewer line clog - often caused by tree roots or years of accumulated debris. Professionals typically use camera inspection to locate the blockage precisely before it backs up into the home and turns into a much bigger, smellier disaster.

## Leaking Pipes

Small cracks and pinhole leaks that seem harmless in warmer months can expand significantly once cold winter temperatures set in. Homeowners can check visible pipes under sinks themselves, but leaks hidden behind walls or under floors need a professional eye to find. Left unaddressed, these leaks also create the right conditions for dangerous mold growth over time.

## Water Heater Problems

Mineral deposits from hard water gradually reduce your water heater's hot water capacity and can lower pressure throughout the house. That same buildup clogs faucets, hoses, and pipes elsewhere in the system, dragging down performance well beyond just your water heater.
