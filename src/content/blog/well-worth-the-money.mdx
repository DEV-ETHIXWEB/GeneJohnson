## A Decade of Trust

Some of the best praise a company can get comes from customers who keep calling back year after year. Alan has been using Gene Johnson Heating, Cooling, Plumbing and Electrical for years to service the furnace in his home, and over the last decade our team has handled at least four sewer repairs for him as well.

## Why He Keeps Coming Back

Alan is upfront that our pricing runs a little higher than some competitors, but for him that's beside the point. "The prices are a little higher than normal but you get what you pay for," he told us. "I'd rather get a couple of extra dollars and know its going to get right the first time."

That's the trade-off we ask every customer to consider: a slightly higher rate in exchange for work that's done correctly the first time, without callbacks or surprises.

## What Stood Out

Alan singled out two things in particular — the depth of expertise our technicians bring to the job, and how consistently we show up on time. For a homeowner who has trusted the same company with both furnace maintenance and multiple sewer repairs over ten years, punctuality and know-how aren't small details; they're the reason he never bothered shopping around.

Stories like Alan's are why we stand behind every repair, whether it's a furnace tune-up or a full [sewer line repair](/sewer-line-repair-replacement/). If you're weighing quotes and wondering whether the extra cost is worth it, we'd say the same thing Alan did: it is.
