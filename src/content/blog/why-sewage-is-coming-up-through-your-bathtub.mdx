Sewage backing up through a bathtub drain is one of the more alarming things a homeowner can encounter, and for good reason - it's usually a sign of a problem well beyond the tub itself.

## What's usually causing it

**Clogged drain pipes.** A buildup of hair, grease, and debris further down the line can eventually force sewage backward, and the tub - often the lowest fixture in the house - is where it shows up first.

**Tree root infiltration.** Roots find their way into sewer pipes through small cracks and joints, growing until they block the line completely.

**A collapsed or broken sewer line.** Aging pipes crack or fail outright due to ground settling, age, or nearby construction vibration.

**Heavy rain or flooding.** When municipal sewer systems get overwhelmed, sewage can be forced backward into home plumbing systems connected to it.

**A main sewer line blockage.** Sometimes the problem isn't your home's plumbing at all, but an issue in the city line that's affecting several properties at once.

## How to narrow it down

Check other drains in the house to see if the problem is isolated or widespread. Look outside for sinkholes or unusually green, lush patches of lawn - both can indicate a damaged section of pipe underground. Gurgling sounds from other fixtures and any correlation with recent heavy rain are also useful clues. When self-diagnosis isn't conclusive, a professional [sewer camera inspection](/sewer-line-repair-replacement/) will show exactly what's going on.

## Why you shouldn't wait

Raw sewage carries bacteria, viruses, and other biohazards capable of causing E. coli infection, hepatitis, and respiratory illness. Beyond the health risk, it can cause structural damage, mold growth, lingering odors, and a real hit to your property's value.

## What to do right now

Stop using water throughout the house immediately, keep people and pets away from the affected area, increase ventilation, and wear protective gear if you must be near the contaminated area. Then call for [emergency plumbing service](/emergency-plumbing/) - this isn't a problem to sit on overnight.
