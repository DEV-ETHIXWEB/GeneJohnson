Most of us spend up to 90% of our time indoors, and the air inside a home can actually be more polluted than the air outside because of trapped dust, debris, and household chemicals. Your air ducts play a bigger role in that than you might think.

## How Ducts Affect What You Breathe

Your HVAC system's ductwork distributes heated and cooled air to every room in your house. That's efficient when the air is clean - but when dust, pet dander, or other contaminants build up inside the ducts, the system spreads them just as effectively. Over time that can contribute to respiratory irritation, sinus problems, and worse allergy or asthma symptoms for household members.

## When to Actually Clean Them

There's no strict calendar rule here - the right time to clean your ducts is whenever they show signs of buildup. Watch for:

- Visible dust blowing out of vents when the system kicks on
- Excessive dust settling around the house even after regular cleaning
- Vent covers and grills that look dirty or discolored

If you're noticing any of these, it's a sign your ductwork needs attention rather than something to put off.

## What to Do Between Cleanings

Regular HVAC maintenance keeps buildup from getting out of hand in the first place. The single most important habit is checking your air filter every few months and replacing it once it's visibly dirty - the filter is the first line of defense against contaminants entering your ducts at all.
