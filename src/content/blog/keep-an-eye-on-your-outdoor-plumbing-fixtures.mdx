Outdoor plumbing fixtures rarely get a second thought until something goes wrong — and by the time you notice, the damage may already be done. With summer keeping everyone busy, it's easy to let outdoor faucets, spigots, and drains slide right past your attention.

## Signs of a Problem

A few warning signs are worth checking for regularly:

- Leaky pipes or visibly rusted connections
- Low or inconsistent water pressure from outdoor faucets
- Puddling in the yard where standing water has no business being

Cracked pipes are especially worth catching early — left alone, they can cause devastating damage inside your walls once the problem migrates indoors.

## Simple Habits That Help

Check your outdoor faucets periodically to make sure they're delivering steady, adequate flow. Cover drains and spigots when they're not in use to keep dirt and debris from building up inside them, which is one of the most common causes of outdoor plumbing blockages.

## When It's Time to Replace, Not Repair

Extensive rust, visible mold growth, hard water scale buildup, persistently low pressure, or a seal that keeps leaking no matter how many times it's tightened are all signs that a fixture needs to be replaced rather than patched again.
