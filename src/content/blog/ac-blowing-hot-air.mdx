Few things are more frustrating on a hot day than an AC that's running but not actually cooling anything. Here are the five most common reasons that happens.

## 1. A Coolant Leak

Refrigerant is what makes heat transfer possible in your system. When levels drop because of a leak, the unit simply can't cool the air the way it should. If you hear a hissing sound near the unit, that's a sign of a serious leak that needs professional attention right away.

## 2. A Frozen Unit

Setting your thermostat below 72°F can cause the system to freeze up internally. If you suspect this is happening, shut the unit off for a couple of hours to let it thaw completely before running it again.

## 3. A Clogged Filter

A dirty air filter blocks proper airflow, forcing your unit to work harder and cool less effectively. Replacing the filter monthly is one of the simplest things you can do to keep your [AC running efficiently](/cooling/ac-maintenance/).

## 4. A Tripped Breaker

Power surges can flip your AC's breaker to the off position without you noticing. Check the breaker panel and reset it if needed - but if it keeps tripping repeatedly, that's a sign of a deeper issue that needs a professional look.

## 5. A Restricted Condenser

Dirty condenser fins or overgrown vegetation around the outdoor unit can choke off the airflow it needs to work. Rinsing the fins with a garden hose and trimming back nearby plants can often restore normal function.
