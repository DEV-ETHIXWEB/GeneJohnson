Thanksgiving prep puts your kitchen - and your garbage disposal - through more than it sees the rest of the year. A few precautions ahead of the big meal can save you from an emergency call while your guests are already at the table.

## Keep Utensils Out

It sounds obvious, but in the chaos of holiday cooking, forks, spoons, and knives find their way into the disposal more often than you'd think. Once the blades are running, a stray utensil can wrap around them, jamming the gears or damaging the motor outright. That's not a quick fix - it usually means a professional repair or a full replacement.

## Don't Overload It

A big holiday meal means a lot of scraps in a short window of time. Feeding your [garbage disposal](/garbage-disposals/) too much at once is one of the fastest ways to clog your drain pipes or break the blades. Feed scraps in gradually, running water the whole time, rather than dumping everything in after the meal is done.

## Skip the Starchy Foods

Potatoes, rice, and pasta are especially bad for disposals. They expand when they absorb water, and a disposal doesn't fully break them down - so what goes in as small scraps can swell into a slow, progressive clog further down the line. The good news is that this particular problem is relatively easy to fix compared to a jammed motor or broken blade, but it's still an easy one to avoid altogether by tossing starchy scraps in the trash or compost instead.

## A Little Care Goes a Long Way

None of this requires special equipment - just some awareness in the middle of a busy cooking day. Treat your disposal carefully during the rush, and it'll be ready for dessert cleanup without a hitch.
