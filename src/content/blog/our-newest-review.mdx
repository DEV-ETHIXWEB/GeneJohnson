Some relationships with a home services company last a season. Others last a lifetime. Diana M.'s review, shared through Customer Lobby, is a great example of the latter.

## Thirty years and counting

Diana wrote: "I have been with Gene Johnson Heating, Cooling, Plumbing and Electrical for about 30 years." That kind of loyalty doesn't happen by accident - it comes from consistently showing up and doing the job right, visit after visit.

## What's kept her coming back

Diana has a service contract with us for $7-8 a month, which reduces the cost of service calls when she needs them. Beyond the savings, she values the familiarity: our team knows her home and her history when she calls, so there's no starting from scratch every time.

As she put it: "I like never have to worry about whether or not the job will be done." That's the kind of trust that takes years to build, and she's since recommended us to her own family.

## Her bottom line

Diana is candid that plumbing and electrical services aren't cheap - but she feels the quality of the work justifies the cost. "I am always happy with their services," she said, summing up three decades of experience in a single line.
