A failing sewer line rarely announces itself all at once. Instead, it tends to show up as a handful of smaller symptoms that are easy to dismiss individually but add up to a clear pattern. Here are the three biggest warning signs to watch for.

## Sign #1: Sluggish Drainage Everywhere

A clog in your main sewer line affects every fixture connected to it, not just one. If every toilet in the house is slow to clear and every drain seems to empty more slowly than usual, that's a sign the problem is in the main line rather than in a single fixture. Partial clogs like this tend to get worse over time, not better, so it's worth having a professional take a look rather than waiting to see if it resolves on its own.

## Sign #2: Sewage Signs in the Yard

Keep an eye on your yard for puddles that smell like sewage, unexplained odors, or patches of grass that are unusually green and lush compared to the rest of the lawn. That lushness is often a sign of excess moisture and nutrients from a leaking underground line — worth a professional look before it gets worse.

## Sign #3: Sewage Backing Up Into the Home

This is the most serious sign, and it should be treated as an emergency. Sewage backing up into sinks, tubs, or floor drains exposes your home and your family to contamination, and it calls for a 24-hour professional response.
