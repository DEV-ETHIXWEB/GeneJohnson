If an outlet in your home has started humming or buzzing, that's not something to shrug off. It's usually a sign of a real electrical problem that needs attention soon.

## What's Causing the Noise

Loose wiring connections inside the outlet box are usually the cause of buzzing or humming sounds. Other common culprits include:

- Loose or damaged wiring, especially in older homes
- Overloaded circuits from high-wattage appliances
- Faulty outlets or plugs
- Improper grounding
- Issues at the breaker panel itself

## Why It's Dangerous

A buzzing outlet carries three real risks: it can start a fire from heat buildup, it can shock you if live current is exposed, and it can damage the appliances plugged into it. Buzzing outlets pose a legitimate shock hazard, not just an annoyance.

## What to Do Right Now

1. Unplug everything from the affected outlet
2. Check whether the outlet cover feels warm to the touch
3. Don't use the outlet again until it's been professionally inspected

## Preventing It Going Forward

- Avoid overloading any single outlet with multiple high-draw devices
- Have old or outdated wiring updated
- Schedule regular professional electrical inspections
- Consider AFCI or GFCI protection where it's not already installed
