A slow-draining or fully clogged bathtub is one of the most common plumbing headaches, and thankfully, one of the more fixable ones. Most bathtub clogs come down to accumulated hair, soap scum, or the occasional kid's toy, though a persistent or recurring clog can sometimes signal something further down the line, like roots infiltrating the sewer line. A quick visual check of the drain, and a sniff for any sewage odor, will usually tell you which situation you're dealing with.

## Try a Plunger First

A cup-style plunger is often the fastest fix. Block the overflow drain with a wet rag so you get full suction, then plunge hard for 15 to 20 seconds before checking your progress. Pouring boiling water down the drain beforehand can help loosen whatever's causing the blockage.

## Use a Drain Snake

If the plunger doesn't clear it, a drain snake can physically reach and remove trapped material. Feed it down the pipe, rotate it when you hit resistance, and pull out whatever comes up. It sometimes takes more than one pass to fully clear a stubborn clog.

## Chemical Cleaners — With Caution

Chemical drain cleaners can work, but they rely on caustic ingredients that demand real caution: good ventilation, gloves, and eye protection. These should be an occasional tool, not a regular habit — frequent use can actually damage your pipes over time.

## Natural Alternatives

A baking soda and vinegar combination, an enzyme-based drain cleaner, boiling water, or even a straightened wire hanger can clear lighter clogs without the risk that comes with harsh chemicals.

## Keeping It From Coming Back

- Install a drain hair catcher in every tub and shower
- Run a weekly baking soda and vinegar treatment as routine maintenance
- Avoid letting anything other than water and soap go down the drain

## When to Call a Plumber

If none of these methods work, or the clog keeps coming back no matter what you try, it's worth having a professional take a look — a recurring clog is often a sign of a deeper blockage that DIY tools simply can't reach.
