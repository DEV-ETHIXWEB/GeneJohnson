Getting your HVAC system ready for fall doesn't require any special expertise - just a willingness to follow a few simple habits before the weather turns. Here are seven mistakes we see homeowners make every year, and how to avoid them.

## 1. Leaving All Vents Wide Open

It seems logical to keep every vent open, but directing airflow to the rooms you actually use is more efficient than heating empty spaces. The one exception: don't close vents near your thermostat, since that can trick the system into overcompensating.

## 2. Neglecting Air Filters

A clogged filter restricts airflow and drags down indoor air quality. Change filters monthly, or at minimum inspect them regularly for visible debris and damage.

## 3. Skipping a Visual Inspection

Take a few minutes to listen for unusual sounds and check for leaks around your unit. Catching a small issue early is a lot cheaper than catching it after it's become a major repair.

## 4. Leaving the Thermostat on Summer Settings

As temperatures shift outside, your [thermostat](/thermostats/) settings should shift too. Running fall temperatures slightly cooler than summer settings reduces strain on the system as it transitions to heating mode.

## 5. Letting the Condenser Get Cluttered

Keep the area around your outdoor condenser clear, and avoid planting trees or shrubs too close to it. Obstructions compromise airflow and reduce efficiency.

## 6. Ignoring Ductwork

Seal any leaks or cracks in your ducts before cold weather sets in. Otherwise you're paying to heat air that never makes it into your living space.

## 7. Skipping Regular Professional Maintenance

Professional inspections extend the life of your system and catch problems before they turn into costly repairs. It's the single most effective habit on this list.
