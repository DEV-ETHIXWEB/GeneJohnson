Not every heating system works the same way, and radiant heating is one of the more overlooked options for Seattle-area homes. Rather than pushing warm air through ductwork the way a forced-air furnace does, radiant systems rely on heated coils or tubes filled with heated liquid that run through panels beneath the floor or behind the walls or ceiling.

## Better Efficiency

Forced-air heating loses some warmth as it travels through ductwork on its way to each room. Radiant systems skip that step entirely, delivering warmth directly to the space without that energy loss along the way.

## Cleaner Air

Traditional forced-air systems can dry out a home's air in winter while circulating dust through filters and ductwork with every cycle. Radiant heating avoids both problems, which makes it a particularly good option for households dealing with allergies or sensitive respiratory systems.

## More Comfortable, Quieter Heat

There's no cold floor to deal with, no cycling noise as the system kicks on and off, and heat is distributed evenly across a room rather than in the uneven bursts typical of forced air. It also doesn't dry out skin the way blown heat often does.

## Built to Last

Once installed, a radiant heating system is designed to last for many years with minimal fuss, making it a solid long-term investment for the right home.
