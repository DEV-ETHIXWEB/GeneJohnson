A bathroom refresh doesn't have to mean a full remodel. Some of the biggest visual improvements come from small, inexpensive changes - and when you're ready for something bigger, a few targeted upgrades go a long way.

## Start Simple

- **Add storage.** Shelves and cabinets that match your existing décor keep everyday items organized instead of cluttering the counter.
- **Use mason jars or vials** on the vanity to store cotton balls, swabs, and other small essentials in a way that looks intentional.
- **Swap the cabinet hardware.** New knobs and pulls are one of the cheapest ways to make a bathroom feel updated.

## Bring in the Professionals

- **Replace an outdated tub surround.** A modern surround can transform the whole room's look in a single project.
- **Upgrade the vanity.** A new vanity is a budget-friendly way to modernize a bathroom without redoing the whole layout.
- **Retile the floor.** New flooring, especially tile, is one of the most noticeable upgrades a bathroom can get.

## Round It Out

- **Choose paint that complements your cabinetry** and existing fixtures rather than fighting against them.
- **Replace old towel racks** - dated or corroded hardware stands out more than most people realize.
- **Hang a new mirror** to add visual interest and freshen the whole room.
- **Update your window treatments** for a completely different feel with very little effort.
