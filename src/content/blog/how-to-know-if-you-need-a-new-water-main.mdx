Your water main is the line that carries fresh water from the city supply into your home, and when it starts to fail, the signs aren't always obvious at first. Catching the warning signs early can mean the difference between a straightforward repair and a catastrophic one.

## Signs Your Water Main May Be Failing

**Water bubbling up from the ground** around your home is one of the clearest signals of a burst main line, and it calls for immediate professional attention.

**Unexplained puddles or wet patches** in the yard or driveway - especially isolated ones with no obvious source like rain or sprinklers - often point to an underground leak.

**Rising water bills** without a change in usage are worth investigating. The leak may not be visible at all if it's happening underneath the home, inside a wall, or in the basement.

**Mold or mildew** forming in the basement can be a sign of ongoing moisture intrusion from a hidden leak, and left unchecked it can eventually damage your foundation as trapped water expands and contracts with temperature changes.

**Cracking near the foundation** is another red flag, particularly if it appears alongside any of the signs above.

**Reduced water pressure**, whether at one faucet or throughout the house, can indicate a leak somewhere along the line feeding your home.

## What to Do Next

If any of these sound familiar, don't wait for the problem to get worse. A licensed plumber can perform leak detection to confirm whether your water main is the issue and recommend the right next step, whether that's a targeted repair or a full [repiping](/repiping/) of the affected section.
