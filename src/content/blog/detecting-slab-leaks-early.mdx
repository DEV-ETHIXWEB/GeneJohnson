A slab leak happens when a water line buried beneath your home's concrete foundation cracks or breaks. Because the pipe is hidden, these leaks often go unnoticed until real damage has already been done - which is exactly why catching one early matters so much.

## Why slab leaks happen

Pressure changes, corrosion, and shifting soil can all weaken a pipe over time until it fails. In older copper systems especially, corrosion is a common culprit, and it tends to worsen with age.

## Early warning signs to watch for

- An unexplained jump in your water bill
- Warm spots appearing on your flooring
- The sound of water running when every fixture is off
- Moisture, mildew, or damp patches showing up on surfaces
- Visible cracks forming in flooring or walls
- Reduced water pressure throughout the house

Any one of these on its own might have another explanation, but a couple showing up together is a strong signal to have your plumbing checked.

## Why Seattle homes are particularly exposed

Our wet climate contributes in a few ways: pipe corrosion, especially in older copper systems; soil shifting after heavy rainfall; installation shortcuts from decades past; pressure stress on aging pipe; and tree roots working their way into small existing cracks.

## What happens if it goes unnoticed

Left alone, a slab leak can erode the foundation, warp flooring, encourage mold growth, inflate your water bill month after month, and eventually lead to a sudden, more serious pipe failure.

## How professionals find them

Rather than tearing up your floor to look, plumbers use acoustic listening devices, thermal imaging cameras, moisture meters, and video inspection tools to pinpoint a leak's location with minimal disruption.

## Repair options and prevention

Depending on severity, a spot repair may be enough, or a full [repiping](/repiping/) may make more sense long-term. Annual inspections, water pressure regulation, and simply keeping an eye on your bill for unexplained spikes all help catch a slab leak before it becomes a foundation problem. If you're seeing any of the warning signs above, [schedule a slab leak inspection](/slab-leak-repair/) rather than waiting to see if it gets worse.
