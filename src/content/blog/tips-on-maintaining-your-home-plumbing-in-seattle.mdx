Good plumbing maintenance doesn't require a toolbox full of specialized gear — mostly it requires paying attention. Here are the habits that make the biggest difference for Seattle homeowners.

## Watch for Early Trouble

Get in the habit of glancing over pipes and fixtures around your home. Catching a small drip or a bit of corrosion early is far cheaper than waiting for it to become a full leak or burst pipe.

## Know Where Your Shutoff Valves Are

Before you need them in an emergency is the time to learn where your fixture shutoff valves are located. When something does go wrong, being able to stop the water flow immediately can be the difference between a minor cleanup and significant water damage.

## Keep Drains Clear

Run hot water through your drains regularly to keep buildup from forming. For a deeper clean, a mixture of white vinegar and baking soda followed by hot water five minutes later works well without resorting to harsh chemical cleaners.

## Choose Gentle Cleaners

Biodegradable detergents are easier on your pipes than harsh chemical drain cleaners, which can degrade pipe material over repeated use.

## Service Your Water Heater

Flushing your [water heater](/water-heaters/) every two years clears out sediment and keeps it running at peak efficiency, which also helps it last longer.

## Watch Your Yard

Soft, unexplained mud spots in the yard or garden can be a sign of an underground pipe leak. It's an easy thing to miss, but worth checking if you notice standing water or unusually lush patches of grass in one spot.
