Fall is the hinge point between summer and winter — the ideal window to prep your home's systems before cooler temperatures and heavier rain arrive. Here's a practical checklist to work through.

## Outdoor: protect the AC condenser

As temperatures drop, it's tempting to throw a full cover over your outdoor AC condenser. Don't. Completely covering a condenser in such a manner could hurt your condenser and even make it more appealing to rodents looking for a warm place to nest. Instead, place a temporary covering only on top to keep leaves out while still allowing airflow underneath.

## Indoor: swap your furnace filter

As the seasons transition and your furnace starts running more, filters clog faster than you'd expect. Switching out to a new filter as often as once a month keeps the system running efficiently and improves indoor air quality. If you run a humidifier, clean or replace its filter too — mineral deposits build up over the summer and contribute to dry, uncomfortable winter air if left unaddressed.

## Bring in the professionals for the bigger jobs

Duct cleaning and a comprehensive HVAC inspection aren't do-it-yourself projects. Hiring an experienced company ensures the work is done correctly the first time, which matters more once winter weather actually arrives and you're depending on the system daily.

## Why fall is the time to do this

Waiting until the first cold snap to think about your HVAC system means competing with everyone else for appointments, and often discovering problems only after they've already caused discomfort.
