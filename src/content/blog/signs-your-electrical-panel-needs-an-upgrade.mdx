Electrical panels don't fail dramatically most of the time - they just quietly become inadequate for the home they're serving, until something you want to add (an EV charger, a heat pump, a hot tub) reveals the shortfall.

## Capacity signs

- **Breakers trip regularly**, especially when running multiple appliances at once
- **You want to add an EV charger, heat pump, or generator** and don't have available capacity
- **Your panel is rated 60 or 100 amps** - most modern homes need 150-200 amps for comfortable headroom
- **You're using a lot of extension cords or power strips** as a permanent solution rather than a temporary one

## Safety signs

- **Warm or discolored breakers or panel cover**
- **A burning smell near the panel**
- **Federal Pacific Electric (FPE) or Zinsco panels** - both brands have well-documented breaker failure issues and are commonly flagged in home inspections
- **Visible rust, corrosion, or double-tapped breakers** (two wires under one breaker terminal)

## Why this matters beyond convenience

An undersized or failing panel isn't just inconvenient - overloaded and improperly protected panels are a leading cause of electrical fires. If your panel is showing any of the safety signs above, that's worth addressing regardless of whether you're planning any new additions.

A [panel replacement or upgrade](/panel-replacements-upgrades/) is also frequently the first step before adding an [EV charger](/electric-vehicle-chargers/) or a [standby generator](/whole-home-automatic-standby-generators/), since both typically need more available capacity than an older panel can safely provide.
