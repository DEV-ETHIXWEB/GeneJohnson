Modern homes run on more USB-powered devices than ever — smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, fitness trackers, security cameras, robot vacuums. If your home is still relying on bulky wall-wart chargers plugged into standard outlets, USB outlets are one of the simplest upgrades you can make.

## The convenience factor

Digging through a drawer for the right charger, or fighting for space at a crowded outlet, gets old fast. USB outlets completely eliminate that hassle. With charging ports built right into the wall, you just plug in and go — no adapter required.

## Built for old and new devices

Most quality USB outlets include both USB-A and USB-C ports, so they can charge older devices and newer ones side by side without needing separate adapters for each.

## Efficiency built in

Good USB outlets include smart chips that automatically cut power once a device reaches full charge, which reduces the small but constant energy waste of gear left plugged in around the clock.

## They look better, too

Compared to a nightstand tangled in cords and bulky adapters, a sleek USB outlet in a modern finish is simply a cleaner look — and there are enough style options now to match almost any décor.

## What to look for

When choosing USB outlets, look for a mix of USB-A and USB-C ports, higher amperage (2+ amps handles tablets and laptops, not just phones), surge protection, overload protection, and tamper-resistant designs if you have kids in the house.
