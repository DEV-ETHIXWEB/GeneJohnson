Staying warm through a Pacific Northwest winter doesn't have to mean cranking the heat and hoping for the best. A few smart upgrades and habits can keep your home comfortable while keeping your energy bills in check.

## 1. Upgrade the Thermostat

A programmable [thermostat](/thermostats/) makes temperature management effortless — many models can even be controlled remotely from your phone. Zone heating is another energy-efficient tool worth considering, letting you direct more heat to the rooms you actually use instead of paying to warm empty spaces.

## 2. Call the Professionals

A furnace tune-up before winter hits is one of the best investments you can make. Professional maintenance typically includes a filter change, a thorough cleaning, and an inspection for wear or damage. Catching small issues early keeps long-term repair costs down and gives you peace of mind on the coldest nights.

## 3. Winterize Your House

A few targeted fixes go a long way:

- Check insulation throughout the home, especially in older houses where heat loss is common.
- Apply window film — an easy DIY project where you simply tape film to the window frame to cut down on heat escaping through the glass.
- Keep your fireplace flue closed when it's not in use so warm air doesn't escape straight up the chimney.

## 4. Install a Whole-Home Humidifier

Furnace heat strips moisture from the air, which is why winter often brings dry skin, chapped lips, and static. A whole-home humidifier restores that moisture, protecting your comfort as well as your wood floors and furniture from drying out.
