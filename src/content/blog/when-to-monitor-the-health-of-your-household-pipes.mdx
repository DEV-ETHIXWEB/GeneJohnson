Cold weather puts real stress on household pipes, and the risk of a freeze-related burst climbs right along with the temperature drop. Knowing the early warning signs means catching a problem while it's still a repair, not a flood.

## Sign #1: Low Water Pressure

A pressure drop that affects the whole house — not just one faucet — is a serious signal, and it's more likely to point to a burst pipe once the weather turns cold. If you notice this, it's worth a professional inspection rather than waiting to see if it resolves on its own.

## Sign #2: Discolored Water

Discoloration can come from rust building up inside damaged pipes, or from dirt getting into the system through a break in an underground line. Either way, it calls for prompt professional attention rather than a wait-and-see approach.

## Sign #3: Puddles of Water

Standing water in exposed areas that keeps reappearing after you clean it up is a strong sign of an active leak or burst pipe. This is exactly the kind of issue [leak detection](/emergency-plumbing/) services are built to pinpoint quickly, before the damage spreads.

## Sign #4: An Unusually High Water Bill

Water loss from a burst pipe doesn't always show up visually first — sometimes the first clue is your bill. Any inexplicable increase in your monthly water costs during winter should be addressed right away rather than chalked up to a one-off fluke.

## Don't Wait Out the Winter

These four signs are worth checking on a regular basis through the cold months, not just when something already looks wrong.
