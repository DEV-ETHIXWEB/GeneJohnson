A slab leak is exactly what it sounds like: a leaking water pipe running beneath the concrete slab your house is built on. Because the leak is hidden under the floor, it can go undetected for a long time - quietly damaging flooring, walls, furniture, and anything else in its path. Catching it early is almost entirely a matter of knowing what to look for.

## Rising Water Bills

If your water bill jumps noticeably without an obvious explanation, like a longer shower schedule or extra house guests, a hidden leak is one of the first things worth ruling out.

## Damp Flooring or Carpeting

Wet spots on the floor or in carpeting, especially ones that keep coming back after drying, usually mean water has already worked its way up from below. This is worth getting checked right away rather than waiting to see if it resolves on its own.

## Warm Spots on the Floor

If the leak is on your hot water line, you may actually feel a warm patch on the floor in an otherwise unheated room. Beyond the direct damage to your flooring, a hot water leak also forces your water heater to run more than it should, driving up energy costs in the process.

## Lower Water Pressure

When water is escaping through a slab leak before it reaches your fixtures, you'll typically notice less water pressure at your faucets and showerheads than usual.

## What Causes a Slab Leak

Most slab leaks trace back to one of two things: corrosion in aging pipes, which is more common in older homes, or gradual abrasion, where pipes rub against concrete or rock as they naturally expand and contract with temperature changes. Over years, that friction wears a small point of damage that eventually turns into a leak.

## What to Do If You Suspect One

Slab leaks only get more expensive to fix the longer they run undetected - moisture works its way into flooring, subfloor, and eventually structural elements of the home. If you notice any combination of these signs, it's worth having a professional perform [slab leak detection and repair](/slab-leak-repair/) rather than waiting for more obvious damage to appear.
