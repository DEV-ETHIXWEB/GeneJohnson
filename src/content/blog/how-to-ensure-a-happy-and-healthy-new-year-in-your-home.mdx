A new year is a natural time to take stock of the habits that affect your home's health, and plumbing plays a bigger role in that than most people realize. Here are four simple changes that can make a real difference.

## Consider a Water Filtration System

Installing a water filtration system helps protect your family against the chemicals and contaminants that can show up in drinking water. Whether it's a water softener, a reverse osmosis system, or carbon-based filtration, the right setup for your home means you're no longer relying on bottled water to feel confident about what's coming out of the tap.

## Leave Drain Cleaning to the Professionals

It's tempting to reach for a store-bought drain cleaner at the first sign of a slow drain, but those products often cause more harm than good. Household cleaning chemicals contain harsh irritants that can affect skin and respiratory health, and they can also damage older pipes over repeated use. A professional [drain cleaning](/drains/) gets the job done without introducing those risks into your home.

## Flush Your Water Heater

Sediment and lime-scale build up inside a water heater tank over time, reducing efficiency and shortening its lifespan. A regular flush clears that buildup out and helps keep hot water flowing consistently throughout the house. Learn more about maintaining your [water heater](/water-heaters/) here.

## Clean Your Faucets Regularly

It's an easy step to overlook, but a monthly faucet cleaning helps prevent bacteria and microbes from accumulating around the sink area, cutting down on germ transmission for the whole household.
