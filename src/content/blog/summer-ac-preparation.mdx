Before the first real heat wave hits, it's worth taking a few minutes to make sure your air conditioner is ready for the season ahead.

## Know the Warning Signs

Most air conditioning units last at least 10 years, depending on the quality and brand, but that doesn't mean you should wait for a total breakdown to pay attention. Watch for an inability to cool your home evenly, strange noises, musty odors, pooling water near the unit, or noticeably weaker airflow. These are red flags worth acting on, not ignoring.

## Don't Skip Annual Maintenance

Dust and dirt build up inside your system over time, forcing it to work harder than it should. A professional annual inspection improves energy efficiency and gives a technician the chance to catch small issues - a worn part, a refrigerant leak - before they turn into a full breakdown in the middle of a heat wave. Our [AC maintenance](/cooling/ac-maintenance/) visits are built around exactly this.

## Let Your Thermostat Work for You

Keeping your thermostat as high as you can comfortably stand during summer months makes a real difference - every degree you raise it conserves meaningful energy. Around 78°F tends to be the sweet spot between comfort and efficiency.

## A Few More Easy Wins

- Run fans to keep air circulating so the space feels cooler at a higher setting
- Close drapes during the day to block direct heat gain
- Open windows at night once outdoor temperatures drop enough to skip the AC altogether
