Keeping a furnace running reliably through a cold winter doesn't take much - just a handful of habits, done consistently. Here are four worth building into your routine.

## 1. Replace Air Filters Regularly

Your filter's job is to trap airborne contaminants before they circulate through your home, but a clogged filter restricts airflow and forces the furnace to work harder than it should. Change it before it gets too dirty - as a rule of thumb, plan on a replacement every couple of months during heating season.

## 2. Schedule a Yearly Professional Tune-Up

A professional visit does more than a filter change ever could: cleaning the system, making performance adjustments, and running a comprehensive inspection to catch developing issues early. That kind of preventative attention improves comfort and efficiency while extending the life of the unit and lowering the odds of a mid-winter breakdown.

## 3. Have Your Ducts Cleaned

Dust, dirt, mold, and pollen accumulate inside ductwork over time, whether you notice it or not. That buildup obstructs airflow and forces your [furnace](/furnace-repair-and-maintenance/) to use more energy to move the same amount of air. Professional duct cleaning every two to three years keeps the system running at peak efficiency.

## 4. Seal Household Air Leaks

Small gaps around windows and doors let warm air escape more than most homeowners realize, and your furnace has to work harder to compensate for that constant loss. Caulking and weatherstripping from any hardware store are inexpensive fixes that meaningfully reduce both strain on the system and your energy bill.
