## A Four-Year Relationship

Korry has been a Gene Johnson Heating, Cooling, Plumbing and Electrical customer for about four years, using our service plan for a rental property he owns. Being on a plan comes with a built-in discount, but that's not the only reason Korry has stuck with us.

## Responsive When It Counts

"They are very responsive, and their price is competitive," Korry told us. For a landlord managing a rental property, responsiveness matters as much as price - a slow callback on a maintenance issue can mean an unhappy tenant or a small problem turning into a bigger one.

## Why a Service Plan Makes Sense for Rental Properties

Korry's experience points to a pattern we see often with property owners: a maintenance plan takes the guesswork out of upkeep. Instead of waiting for something to break, scheduled visits catch small issues early, and the membership discount takes some of the sting out of the repairs that do come up.
