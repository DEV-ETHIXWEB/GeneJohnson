Getting a new [garbage disposal](/garbage-disposals/) installed is only half the story - keeping it clean and odor-free is what makes it worth having. Here are a few simple habits that will keep your new unit running well for years to come.

## Try Baking Soda and Vinegar

A cup of baking soda followed by a splash of vinegar creates a fizzing reaction that helps break down debris clinging to the blades and the inside of the drain. Run water through the disposal first, let the mixture bubble for a few minutes, then rinse it clean. Doing this weekly is an easy, low-cost habit to build.

## Use Citrus Peels

Lemon, grapefruit, and orange peels are naturally acidic and do a great job of neutralizing odors. Heavy-duty disposals can usually process peels while running; older or lighter-duty units should be treated more like any other food scrap.

## Know What to Avoid

Some foods create outsized odor problems no matter how well you maintain the unit - cabbage, dairy, asparagus, meat, eggs, onions, broccoli, fish, garlic, and anything already spoiled. These are better off in the trash than the disposal.

## Ice Cubes and Salt

Running kosher salt and ice cubes through the disposal together helps scrape away debris clinging to the blades. Follow with a rinse of water to flush everything through.

## When to Call a Professional

If odors persist after trying all of the above, the problem may be further down in the pipes beneath your sink.
