It's easy to think of the toilet as one of the least-changed fixtures in the house, but researchers and manufacturers have been quietly pushing the technology forward in some surprising directions.

## Solar-Powered Toilets

Researchers have developed toilets powered entirely by sunlight that convert waste into usable fuel — producing hydrogen, water, and electricity in the process. The hydrogen can be stored in fuel cells and used to keep the system running even after the sun goes down.

## Waste Disinfection

Another innovation heats waste to more than 200 degrees Celsius to eliminate germs and pathogens, significantly cutting the risk of disease transmission. This kind of technology is especially valuable in regions where sanitation infrastructure struggles to keep up with disease prevention.

## Turning Waste Into Fertilizer

Urine is rich in nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium — nutrients that are essential for agriculture. Some newer toilet systems are designed to convert urine into ash, water, and usable fertilizer, with promising applications for agriculture on a global scale.
