A gurgling sink is annoying more than anything else at first - but it's also your plumbing telling you something specific is off, and it's usually manageable once you know what to look for.

## What's causing the noise

**Trapped air bubbles.** As water moves through the drain pipe, it can create small vacuum pockets that release as an audible gurgle.

**Blockages.** Hair, food particles, and mineral buildup restrict flow and create turbulence, mixing air into the water in a way that produces that bubbling sound.

**Vent pipe issues.** Every drain relies on a vent pipe to let air in and equalize pressure. If that vent is blocked, water flowing through the system creates suction that pulls air backward through your fixtures instead.

**Improper venting or sloping.** In older Seattle homes especially, pipes that weren't installed with correct venting or slope can produce a persistent gurgle that DIY fixes won't fully solve.

## What to try first

A plunger or drain snake handles most minor clogs. Cleaning out the P-trap under the sink often removes the debris causing the noise directly. If you're able to check the roof vent for obvious blockage - leaves, debris, nests - that's worth a look too. Baking soda and vinegar is a reasonably effective, eco-friendly option for lighter buildup.

## When to bring in a professional

If the gurgling doesn't go away, multiple drains start acting up at once, or you notice a foul smell along with it, it's time to call a plumber rather than keep troubleshooting.
