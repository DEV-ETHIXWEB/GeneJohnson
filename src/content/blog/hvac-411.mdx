One of the most common complaints we hear from homeowners is that one room is never quite the right temperature - too warm in summer, too cold in winter, no matter what the thermostat says. It's a reasonable instinct to assume that means your whole HVAC system needs to be repaired or replaced, but there are a few things worth ruling out first.

## Start With a Maintenance Inspection

During a seasonal maintenance visit, a technician can thoroughly inspect your system to identify what's actually causing the inconsistency, including confirming that your thermostat itself is reading temperatures accurately.

## Check for Duct Leaks

Even well-installed ductwork loses some efficiency over longer runs. Sealing gaps and leaks in your ducts limits how much conditioned air escapes before it reaches the room it's meant for, and the job typically only takes a few hours.

## Consider a Wireless Thermostat

Thermostats are often placed wherever was convenient during construction - not necessarily where they'll give the most accurate reading of how your home actually feels. Upgrading to a [wireless thermostat](/thermostats/) system can send more accurate temperature data to your heating and cooling equipment, helping it respond more precisely to what's really happening in each room.

## Ask About Radiant Heating

For rooms that are chronically cold, radiant heating - coils installed in the walls, floors, or baseboards - can provide immediate, targeted warmth with very little ongoing maintenance, and it can be a nice addition to a home's overall value.
